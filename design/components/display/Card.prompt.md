Section container for dashboard panels, detail blocks, and forms.

```jsx
<Card eyebrow="Autoship" title="Next delivery" actions={<Button size="sm" variant="secondary">Reschedule</Button>}>
  …
</Card>
<Card title="Recent orders" flush><DataTable … /></Card>
```

- `tone="cream"` for gentle brand callouts; `tone="brand"` (teal) at most once per screen.
- No colored left borders, no heavy shadows.
